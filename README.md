## What does a China shopping agent do?

A China shopping agent purchases from Chinese sellers on your behalf, receives the goods at a warehouse, and helps arrange international delivery. It can be useful when you need help with ordering, seller communication, checking goods before shipment, or combining purchases from different sellers.

For CFANS, the process has two main stages: **purchase the products and send them to the China warehouse**, then **choose the items and shipping method for your international parcel**. Paying for an item does not complete the international shipping stage.

An agent is most useful when those extra services solve a real problem for your order. If a seller already offers suitable direct delivery and you do not need warehouse inspection or consolidation, compare that option's complete cost and return process too.

## How to buy from China with CFANS

1. **Find the product.** Copy its Taobao, Tmall, 1688 or Weidian link into CFANS, or search by product name.
2. **Check the order.** Select the right options and quantity. Add clear remarks where a size, colour or seller instruction matters.
3. **Pay for the purchase.** Review the product cost, domestic shipping and any selected services. Check the order page for seller changes or supplementary payment requests.
4. **Review warehouse results.** When items arrive, inspect the available QC photos and order information. Raise a visible discrepancy before arranging international shipment.
5. **Plan the parcel.** Choose which stored items to send together or separately, considering packaging, weight, dimensions and route restrictions.
6. **Choose international delivery.** Compare the available shipping options for your destination and parcel, confirm the address, pay the shipping charge and follow tracking.

Use the [official beginner guide](https://www.cfans.com/content/beginner-guide) for the current on-screen steps.

## Compare total cost, QC and shipping before choosing an agent

The quoted purchasing fee is only one part of the decision. Build a budget from **product price + domestic shipping + applicable payment or service charges + optional warehouse services + international shipping + any destination charges not included in the quote**. Count each charge once and check what the selected route includes.

For a useful shipping comparison, keep the item type, destination, parcel weight, dimensions and packaging assumptions consistent. A light but bulky parcel may be charged using volumetric weight. Combining items changes the parcel: compare that result with separate shipments instead of assuming consolidation always costs less.

CFANS describes basic inspection of appearance, visible damage and certain order details. **QC is not product authentication or functional testing**; its help centre states that electronics are not powered on for the basic check. Review the applicable inspection and return terms for the item you are buying.
